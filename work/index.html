Project 01 · Ad-hoc decision support

Modeling of Revenue, Quota and Compensation Impacts After a Workforce Restructuring

After a 40% workforce reduction, the team had less capacity but the same revenue-reporting deadlines. Leadership had less than 2 weeks to reset sales coverage and quotas, but first needed to understand the effects on sales capacity, revenue attribution, pipeline & variable pay. The required context was split across HR data, Salesforce, Snowflake schemas, and compensation plans; the incentive platform was not designed for this unusual one-time scenario.

  • RAG

01 · Technical architecture decision

Design a grounded, reusable scenario architecture

Architecture choice
Chose a modular scenario layer over reconfiguring the production incentive-compensation platform. This protected live payout processing while keeping the model separate from the system of record.
Grounding & orchestration
Used a Claude workflow with RAG to retrieve company-specific compensation rules and MCP integrations to bring the required business context into the analysis.
Validation & reuse
Applied job-to-plan mapping, source-evidence, duplicate-record, and cross-system consistency checks. Parameterized inputs and rules made the framework reusable for future reorganizations.

02 · Sales Ops validation decision

Validate and resolve Sales Ops exceptions before decisions

Validation logic
Compared employment status, reporting hierarchy, account and opportunity ownership, deal stage, and credit logic to surface records disrupted by AE and manager departures.
Exception classification
Classified each mismatch as people and hierarchy, account and opportunity ownership, or revenue credit and compensation.
Review & approval
Routed exceptions to the accountable HR, Sales Ops, or Compensation and Finance partner and held unresolved records out of leadership-ready results until the resolution was confirmed.

03 · Sales & RevOps impact

Reduce planning cost, improve visibility, and prevent downstream errors

Time & cost avoidance
Delivered the initial model in two days, avoiding weeks of production-platform configuration and UAT effort and the associated implementation cost. The reusable framework also reduced the effort required for future what-if analyses.
Decision visibility
Provided before-and-after views of sales capacity, account ownership, revenue credit, pipeline, quota, and payout exposure, with drill-downs by director and manager.
Operational risk reduction
Surfaced stale ownership, missed handoffs, and crediting exceptions before they affected revenue reporting, quota and payout decisions, or seller compensation.
