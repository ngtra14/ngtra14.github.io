Project 01 · Ad-hoc decision support

Modeling of Revenue, Quota and Compensation Impacts After a Workforce Restructuring

After a 40% workforce reduction, the team had less capacity but the same revenue-reporting deadlines. Leadership had less than 2 weeks to reset sales coverage and quotas, but first needed to understand the effects on sales capacity, revenue attribution, pipeline & variable pay. The required context was split across HR data, Salesforce, Snowflake schemas, and compensation plans; the incentive platform was not designed for this unusual one-time scenario.

01 · Technical architecture decision

Build a temporary scenario layer—not a production IC rebuild

Choice
I modeled the restructuring outside the incentive-compensation platform, joining HR hierarchy, Salesforce ownership, Snowflake data, and compensation-plan rules in a separate decision layer.
Tradeoff
This avoided weeks of configuration and UAT and enabled delivery in two days. In exchange, the solution was designed for one-time scenario planning rather than ongoing payout processing.
Controls
RAG supplied company-specific plan rules, while validation checks confirmed job-to-plan mappings, supporting rule evidence, and duplicate records. Conflicting HR, CRM, and ownership data was routed to human review.

02 · Sales Ops policy decision

Separate reporting-line changes from selling-ownership changes

Decision
A manager change did not automatically move account ownership or seller credit. I used coverage and account ownership to determine seller credit, reporting hierarchy for management rollups, and job profile plus sales motion to assign compensation rules.
Governance
When Workday status, reporting hierarchy, Salesforce ownership, and plan rules disagreed, I applied a documented source-of-truth hierarchy and routed unresolved cases to the appropriate business owner.
Operational effect
This prevented administrative reorganizations from incorrectly shifting revenue credit and produced defensible quota, rollup, and payout calculations.

03 · Sales & RevOps impact

Give leaders a decision-ready restructuring view in two days

Delivery speed
Delivered the scenario model and interactive experience in two days—versus weeks for production-platform reconfiguration—within leadership’s two-week planning window.
Planning visibility
Provided before-and-after views of sales capacity, account ownership, revenue credit, pipeline, quota, and payout exposure, with drill-downs by director and manager.
Secure access
EID-based controls let sales leaders access relevant results without circulating unrestricted compensation workbooks or exposing organization-wide employee data.
