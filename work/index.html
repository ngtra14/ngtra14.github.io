Project 01 · Ad-hoc decision support

Modeling of Revenue, Quota and Compensation Impacts After a Workforce Restructuring

After a 40% workforce reduction, the team had less capacity but the same revenue-reporting deadlines. Leadership had less than 2 weeks to reset sales coverage and quotas, but first needed to understand the effects on sales capacity, revenue attribution, pipeline & variable pay. The required context was split across HR data, Salesforce, Snowflake schemas, and compensation plans; the incentive platform was not designed for this unusual one-time scenario.

01 · Technical architecture decision

Build once for an urgent reorg; reuse for future what-if planning

Evolution
Built for one urgent reorganization, the model evolved into a reusable Sales Ops framework for evaluating future changes to coverage, credit, quota, and compensation.
Reusable design
Standardized scenario inputs, business rules, and validation steps made the same framework repeatable across future reorganizations.
Tradeoff
The external decision layer stayed fast and flexible, but every scenario still required refreshed source data and expert review.

02 · Sales Ops validation decision

Protect account handoffs and seller credit with governed validation

Operational risk
Sudden AE and manager departures left stale Salesforce ownership, missed handoffs, and unreliable credit logic.
Validation & routing
The validation layer surfaced ownership, handoff, and crediting exceptions; classified them as people and hierarchy, account and opportunity ownership, or revenue credit and compensation; and routed each to the appropriate domain expert.
Decision control
Unresolved exceptions stayed out of leadership-ready results until the accountable HR, Sales Ops, or Compensation and Finance partner confirmed the resolution—helping prevent missed handoffs and over- or under-crediting.

03 · Sales & RevOps impact

Turn a two-day response into a reusable planning capability

Delivery speed
Delivered the initial scenario model and interactive experience in two days—versus weeks for production-platform reconfiguration—within leadership’s two-week planning window.
Decision visibility
Provided before-and-after views of sales capacity, account ownership, revenue credit, pipeline, quota, and payout exposure, with drill-downs by director and manager.
Ongoing value
What began as a one-time analysis became a reusable framework for future reorganizations and recurring leadership what-if questions.
