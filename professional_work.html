Case study 01 · Sales & Revenue Operations

Revenue and compensation scenario modeling after a workforce restructuring

After a workforce reduction, the team had less capacity while payroll, commission, and revenue-reporting deadlines remained unchanged. Leadership also needed to reset sales coverage and assign next-quarter quotas in less than two weeks.

My roleI designed the analysis approach, translated compensation logic into the model, used AI to accelerate development, validated the outputs, and built the leadership decision experience.

  • 3 daysAnalysis and interactive tool delivered
  • < 2 weeksTime remaining before quota decisions
  • 3 inputsWorkday HR data, Salesforce CRM, and compensation logic

System architecture

A temporary decision system—not a rushed compensation-platform rebuild

The existing incentive platform was designed for established plans. Configuring and testing one-time restructuring logic there would have taken weeks, so I built an isolated scenario layer for analysis and decision support.

01 · Technical decisions

Optimize for speed without making AI the source of truth

  • Model outside the compensation platform. The scenario was urgent and temporary; production configuration and regression testing would not fit the deadline.
  • Create one analysis layer across fragmented systems. A reconstructed hierarchy connected workforce changes to Salesforce ownership, revenue, quotas, and payouts.
  • Separate AI assistance from calculation authority. RAG supplied relevant commission rules to Claude, while source records, explicit business logic, and reconciliation determined the final numbers.
  • Build for exploration. An interactive experience replaced a complex worksheet and made scenario differences easier to inspect.

02 · Domain expert decisions

Translate organizational changes into defensible revenue and compensation logic

  • Identify which reporting relationships changed credit. Not every hierarchy change should alter revenue attribution or compensation.
  • Apply the right rule at the right level. Seller credit and management compensation required different treatment.
  • Make assumptions explicit. Uncertain cases were reviewed against source data and resolved with the relevant partners.
  • Design around the leadership decision. Director and manager drill-downs matched the level at which coverage and quota tradeoffs were being evaluated.

Impact

A decision-ready answer in three days

  • Delivered the analysis and a usable interactive experience before next-quarter quota planning.
  • Gave leaders a way to compare team structures, revenue attribution, quotas, and payouts before decisions were locked.
  • Avoided waiting weeks to configure and test unusual one-time logic in the incentive compensation platform.
  • Combined AI development speed with human ownership of rules, assumptions, validation, and final decisions.
