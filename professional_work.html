Project 01 · Ad-hoc decision support

Ad Hoc Modeling of Revenue, Quota and Compensation Impacts After a Workforce Restructuring

After a 40% workforce reduction, the team had less capacity but the same revenue-reporting deadlines. Leadership had less than 2 weeks to reset sales coverage and quotas, but first needed to understand the effects on revenue attribution and variable compensation. The required context was split across HR data, Salesforce, Snowflake schemas, and compensation plans; the incentive platform was not designed for this unusual one-time scenario.

01 · Technical decision

Choose a fast, grounded analysis architecture

Scenario layer
I modeled the one-time change outside the IC platform because building, configuring and UAT testing would have taken weeks.
Why RAG
RAG retrieved the company-specific compensation plan rules for the sales motion being analyzed. Claude may understand compensation concepts generally, but it doesn’t know the company’s exact plan design or how the rules vary across sales motions.
AI harness
The harness verified that the employee’s job-profile metadata mapped to the expected compensation plan, checked that retrieved passages supported the rules being applied, and flagged duplicate records to prevent double counting. Conflicts across HR hierarchy data, Workday employment status, and Salesforce account ownership were routed to human review.

02 · Domain expert decision

Decide what the new organization meant for credit, quota, and pay

Hierarchy meaning
A changed manager field did not automatically mean revenue should move. I distinguished administrative reporting changes from coverage changes that should alter account ownership, management rollups, or revenue credit.
Rule application
I mapped each employee’s job profile and sales motion to the correct plan, then separated seller credit from management rollups so the model applied the appropriate quota and payout logic at each level.
Exception ownership
When employment status, reporting hierarchy, Salesforce ownership, and compensation rules disagreed, I determined which source governed the decision, documented the assumption, and resolved ambiguous cases with the relevant business owner.

03 · Impact & delivery

Faster delivery, clearer decisions, broader access

AI-enabled productivity
Grounding AI in the relevant rules helped me apply domain expertise faster, compressing analysis and product delivery into two days while keeping judgment and validation with the domain expert.
Decision value
The interactive experience replaced an outdated worksheet and gave leaders a comprehensive before-and-after view of team structure, revenue credit, quota, and payout, with drill-downs by director and manager.
Secure visibility
Employee ID (EID)–based access controls extended relevant insights to the broader sales organization without distributing unrestricted workbooks, preserving confidence that sensitive employee and compensation data remained protected.
