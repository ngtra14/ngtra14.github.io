Project 01 · Ad-hoc decision support

Modeling a workforce restructuring before quota decisions

After a workforce reduction, the team had less capacity but the same payroll, commission, and revenue-reporting deadlines. Leadership had less than 2 weeks to reset sales coverage and quotas, but first needed to understand the effects on revenue attribution and variable compensation. The required context was split across HR data, Salesforce, Snowflake and compensation plans; the incentive platform was not designed for this unusual one-time scenario.

01 · Technical decision

Choose a fast, grounded analysis architecture

Scenario layer
I modeled the one-time change outside the compensation platform because configuring and regression-testing production plans would have taken weeks.
Why RAG
The commission logic already existed in the codebase. Retrieval brought the relevant rules into Claude’s working context without fine-tuning or relying on model memory, while keeping the source logic inspectable.
AI harness
I chose Claude as the development harness because the work required tracing existing code and reasoning across a large retrieved rule context. Workday, Salesforce, explicit compensation logic, and reconciliation—not the model—remained the calculation authority.

02 · Domain expert decision

Translate a new organization into defensible business logic

Revenue credit
I determined which reporting-line changes should alter attribution; not every hierarchy change affected credit.
Compensation
I separated seller-level rules from management compensation scenarios and applied the appropriate logic to each.
Exceptions
I reviewed assumptions against source data and compensation rules, then resolved uncertain cases with the relevant partners.

03 · Impact & delivery

A decision-ready experience in three days

Delivery
The analysis and interactive tool were delivered in three days, with less than two weeks before quota decisions.
Format
Leaders could drill down by director and manager and compare team structure, revenue attribution, quota, and payout before and after the change.
Decision value
The interactive experience replaced an opaque worksheet and made tradeoffs visible before coverage and quota assignments were locked.
